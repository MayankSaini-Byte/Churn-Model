Understanding the Problem

What is Customer Churn?

Churn occurs when customers stop doing business with a company. In the telecom industry, acquiring a new customer costs 5–25× more than retaining an existing one — making churn prediction one of the highest-ROI applications of machine learning.

Acquisition Cost

New customer acquisition costs 5–25× more than retention. Predicting churn allows companies to invest in keeping existing customers instead.

Revenue Impact

A 5% increase in customer retention can boost profits by 25–95%. Churn models help focus retention budgets where they matter most.

Targeted Action

Instead of blanket discounts, ML models identify specific at-risk customers — enabling personalized, cost-effective retention campaigns.
